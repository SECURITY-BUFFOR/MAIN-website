Every operation starts with a business question: **which critical objective must the organization be able to defend?** The scenario, scope, and success criteria are built around that objective — not around producing the longest possible list of findings.

The result is a clear picture of how a real attacker would move through your environment, where your defenses held, and where they need to improve.
